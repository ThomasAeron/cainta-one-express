How do I request a delivery?

Choose Food Express or Online Bazaar on the home page, complete the request form, and submit your pickup and delivery details.

Where do you deliver?

We serve Cainta, Rizal and nearby supported communities. Your available delivery estimate appears after you enter the address.

I cannot access my account.

Use the account page to sign in or create an account. New accounts require a verification code sent to your email.

How does the Online Bazaar work?

Browse available products, or use Create a Shop to submit your seller details and start managing your store.

What should I do about a payment question?

Include your order details and a screenshot or receipt when contacting support so we can investigate quickly.
